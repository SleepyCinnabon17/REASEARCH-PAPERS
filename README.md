## 📚 **About This Repository**

This repository is a curated archive of my **research papers, academic reports, and analytical writing** developed during my university studies.

My work explores the intersection of **technology, language, literature, and society**, reflecting a deep interest in how computational systems and human narratives shape knowledge, culture, and innovation.

From technical explorations in **Artificial Intelligence and computing** to research rooted in **linguistic, literary, and societal questions**, each paper represents an effort to approach complex ideas with careful analysis and interdisciplinary perspective.

---

## ✨ **Research Philosophy**

Research, to me, is not simply gathering information.  
It is a process of **curiosity, interpretation, and dialogue across disciplines**.

My interests include:

- Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning  
- Computational systems and data analysis  
- Linguistics and language structures  
- Literature and narrative analysis  
- Social and interdisciplinary research

Each project aims to balance **technical precision with thoughtful inquiry**.
