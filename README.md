## 📚 **About This Repository**

This repository serves as a curated archive of my **research papers, academic reports, and analytical writing** developed throughout my university studies.

My work sits at the intersection of **technology, language, literature, and social inquiry**, reflecting a strong interest in how computational systems and human narratives shape the way we understand the world.

From technical explorations in **Artificial Intelligence and computing** to research rooted in **cultural, linguistic, and societal questions**, each paper represents an attempt to investigate complex problems through careful analysis and interdisciplinary thinking.

---

## ✨ **Research Philosophy**

Research, to me, is not merely the accumulation of information but a process of **curiosity, interpretation, and dialogue across disciplines**.

My academic interests include:

- Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning  
- Computational thinking and systems design  
- Linguistics and language structures  
- Literature and narrative analysis  
- Social impact and interdisciplinary research

Each project reflects a commitment to **clarity of thought, intellectual rigor, and meaningful inquiry**.
